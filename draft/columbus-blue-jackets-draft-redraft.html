How It Works

Re-Draft Your Team: This tool lets you rebuild your team's draft history from the first two rounds (2010-2026). Click on any alternative player to swap them with the actual pick and see how your redrafted team would look.

Color Coding: Green (Top 33%) Yellow (Middle 33%) Gray (Bottom 33%)

Top Available Players: For each pick, we show the best players who were drafted after that selection in the same year, sorted by a three-season rating based on Hockey Reference Point Shares. We weight Point Shares and games 50/30/20, newest season first. With the newest weight set to 1, the score is weighted Point Shares divided by weighted games plus 20. The extra games add no Point Shares, keeping very small samples conservative. Players below 20 weighted games are marked Limited data. Goalie Point Shares use the existing 0.72 scaling factor.
