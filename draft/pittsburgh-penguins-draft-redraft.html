How It Works

Re-Draft Your Team: This tool lets you rebuild your team's draft history from the first two rounds (2010-2026). In compact view, click a player to inspect their stats, then choose Select player. In expanded view, click an alternative to select them directly. Both views update the same team.

Color Coding: Green (Top 33%) Yellow (Middle 33%) Gray (Bottom 33%)

Top Available Players: For each pick, we show the best players who were drafted after that selection in the same year, sorted by a three-season rating based on Hockey Reference Point Shares. We weight Point Shares and games 50/30/20, newest season first. With the newest weight set to 1, the score is weighted Point Shares divided by weighted games plus 20. The extra games add no Point Shares, keeping very small samples conservative. Players below 20 weighted games are marked Limited data. Goalie Point Shares use the existing 0.72 scaling factor. The bar shows percentile among all players with NHL appearances in the three-season window. A missing season adds no games or points; players with only older data are labeled with their last data season. Players outside the window have No rating. This estimates demonstrated recent performance; career totals through 2025-2026 are shown separately. Career and peak ratings are not included.
