About this collection

Each selection has linked NHL, team or broadcaster coverage identifying a one-handed finish. Game dates, opponents, goalies and rounds were checked against NHL play-by-play. These are post-overtime shootout goals from regular-season games.

This is a curated selection, not a complete list or a ranking. Dates refer to the game, which may differ from the highlight’s publication date. SO means shootout; final scores include the shootout result. Video and highlight images are supplied by the NHL.
